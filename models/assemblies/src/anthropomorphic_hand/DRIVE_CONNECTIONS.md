# Driven joint interfaces

The pulleys must transmit torque into their child frame. A free pulley on a bearing is not a drive connection.

Finger flexion uses a through-shaft with 1 mm nominal radius. Its driven regions have a D flat at +0.75 mm in the local radial X direction (the finger's +Y direction in neutral). Driven bores have radius 1.03 mm and the matching flat at +0.75 mm: the flat is a contact face; the remaining circumference has 0.03 mm radial running clearance. The circular parent bushings remain radius 1.03 mm. The D section continues through the journals and tip, allowing axial withdrawal. The remaining circular surface bears in the parent bushings.

The proximal eye of each driven phalanx now has an integral D-shaped1.03mm bore, verified on all15variants. Its distal eye retains the2.53mm circular bearing seat for the next child.

The universal joint yaw axis cannot have a continuous vertical shaft through the crossing horizontal flex shaft. It uses two independent keyed vertical stub shafts. The dorsal stub carries both drive pulleys and the dorsal carrier hub; the palmar stub supports the opposing hub, supported by the stationary palm bearing. The carrier ribs carry their combined torque into the horizontal flex bearing supports. All stub hardware must stay outside the central flex-shaft envelope.

The tendon planner reserves the complete flex-shaft radius envelope; neither a missing shaft nor a floating drive pulley is an acceptable clearance workaround.
# Axial withdrawal requirement discovered during integration

The first keyed-shaft prototypes have round support bands and round tips. They
fit and transmit torque in the neutral assembly, but a full round band cannot
pass axially through a D bore during disassembly. Final driven shafts require a
continuous D profile through the complete tip and every intervening journal.
The circular bushings can support that D shaft over its remaining circular
surface. The retaining groove may retain its circular root where that root is
inside the D flat; no larger round tip may remain beyond the groove. Head-side
withdrawal must be checked through the real D hubs before explode authoring.
The corrected continuous-D factory passes63 sleeve-withdrawal samples over finger and wrist sizes, with zero intersections. The shaft has a0.008-mm edge blend and a relieved neck below the head, with a smooth transition lying entirely inside the mating D envelope. All104 placed joint-hardware bodies pass strict solid validation. This was a part-design correction, not a repository bug.

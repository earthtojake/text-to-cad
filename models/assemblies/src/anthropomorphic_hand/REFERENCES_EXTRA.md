# Additional primary photographic references

Retrieved 2026-09-06. Four original photographic assets saved in `tmp/anthropomorphic_hand/references/`. Each was visually inspected; none is a generated image, a render, or a duplicate of the existing Grebenstein reference set. These are reference-study copies; attribution below does not imply unrestricted redistribution.

## Shadow wrist yoke

- File: `tmp/anthropomorphic_hand/references/shadow_wrist_yoke.png` — 1660 × 895.
- [Primary source: Shadow Dexterous Hand Series](https://shadowrobot.com/dexterous-hand-series/).
- [Original image](https://shadowrobot.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Shadow-Robot-Co3721HR-copy-2-e1650536420490.png).
- Caption: Shadow Dexterous Hand holding a spanner, with the wrist flexed and the forearm housing visible. Credit: Shadow Robot Company; the page carries its copyright notice.
- Candidate camera: three-quarter radial/palmar view along the forearm; hand points diagonally down-left.
- Visible detail: polished metal wrist yoke, round pivot bosses, cable/tendon fan through the open wrist, housing exit guides, black finger shells. Strong benchmark for the transition from a dense tendon passage to a clean sculpted wrist. The forearm motors are covered; this is not an exposed actuator-array reference.

## Shadow DEX-EE fingertip contact

- File: `tmp/anthropomorphic_hand/references/shadow_dexee_fingertips.jpg` — 2048 × 1366.
- [Primary source: Shadow Sensors](https://shadowrobot.com/sensors/).
- [Image asset](https://shadowrobot.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Shadow-Robot-Sensors-1-2048x1366.jpg).
- Caption: A human fingertip touches the distal sensor of Shadow's research hand. Credit: Shadow Robot Company; the page carries its copyright notice.
- Candidate camera: close three-quarter view across the curled fingers, with the contacted distal segment nearly side-on.
- Visible detail: rounded dark fingertip caps, machined silver surrounds, pad-to-shell seams, small flush fasteners, exposed circular joint faces, and contact scale. This is the strongest new tactile-fingertip beauty reference in this set.

## DLR/HIT Hand II fingertip and nail forms

- File: `tmp/anthropomorphic_hand/references/dlr_hit_ii_palm.jpg` — 4992 × 2808.
- [Primary source: DLR-HIT Hand II](https://www.dlr.de/en/rm/research/robotic-systems/hands/dlr-hit-hand-ii).
- [Original image](https://www.dlr.de/en/rm/images/roboter_und_systeme/hand/hit_ii/ffh_2010_liegend_von_vorne_freigestellt_4992x2808_orig.jpg/@@images/image).
- DLR caption: “DLR-HIT Hand II.” Credit: DLR (CC-BY 3.0).
- Candidate camera: low distal three-quarter view toward the palm, fingers gently curled toward the camera.
- Visible detail: sculpted metallic nail-like faces, dark rounded fingertip inserts, consistent shell parting gaps, counterbores, and a curved thumb silhouette. Broad soft lighting and the close crop make it useful for judging fingertip hierarchy and finish at human scale.

## DLR/HIT Hand II compact drive macro

- File: `tmp/anthropomorphic_hand/references/dlr_hit_ii_drive_macro.jpg` — 4992 × 2808.
- [Primary source: DLR-HIT Hand II](https://www.dlr.de/en/rm/research/robotic-systems/hands/dlr-hit-hand-ii).
- [Original image](https://www.dlr.de/en/rm/images/roboter_und_systeme/hand/hit_ii/ffh_2011_zerlegt_bestandteil_16_9_4992x2808.jpg/@@images/image).
- DLR caption: “Dismantled finger of the DLR/HIT Hand II.” Credit: DLR (CC-BY 3.0).
- Candidate camera: shallow oblique macro across the exposed drive axis, with a large foreground disc and timing-belt pair behind it.
- Visible detail: stacked metal motor/drive discs, bearing and pulley faces, red toothed belt, machined frame shoulders, and amber flex circuits. Strong compact-mechanism finish and layering reference. This is finger drive hardware, not a forearm tendon capstan; do not label it as the latter.

## Selection limits

The new set concentrates on wrist yokes, fingertip/nail hardware, and exposed compact drives. No additional exposed forearm-array photograph was selected: the strongest DLR forearm imagery overlaps the existing references, and the NASA paper's embedded images were too small or were diagrams. Clone's official high-resolution photographs were beautiful but covered the hardware; they were not added. The accessible UW paper URL redirected to a lab page, so no UW paper image was saved. Our CAD model and builder code were not inspected or judged during this task.

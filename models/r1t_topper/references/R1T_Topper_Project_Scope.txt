OVERVIEW
--------
A modular, aircraft-grade carbon fiber canopy topper for the Rivian R1T pickup
truck. The topper completes the R1T's roofline to match the silhouette of the
Rivian R1S SUV, transforming the truck's profile while adding functional,
configurable utility. The launch use case is a ventilated, dog-friendly canopy.
This is the first product in a planned ecosystem of R1T toppers and accessories.

Target retail price: under $5,000
Target market: Rivian R1T owners
Distribution: direct to consumer, Rivian owner community channels


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FORM & FITMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Topper profile follows the R1S roofline from B-pillar rearward, scaled to
  the R1T bed length and bed rail height.

- Rivian R1T is unibody construction. The topper sits flush with both the bed
  rails and the rear of the cab roofline — no gaps, no adapters.

- The master reference geometry is the 2D side-profile spline curve of the R1S
  roofline mapped onto R1T bed dimensions. All mold geometry derives from this
  curve. Rivian collaboration or physical measurement of both vehicles required
  to finalize this spline.

- Approximate R1T bed dimensions: 54" wide x 44" deep. Precise dimensions to
  be confirmed by physical measurement or Rivian-provided CAD data.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MATERIALS & FABRICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Top panel: aircraft-grade carbon fiber, CNC-molded
- Side panel frames: carbon fiber or aluminum extrusion (TBD)
- Modular insert panels: carbon fiber frames with material per module type
- Rear door: carbon fiber
- Hardware: stainless or anodized aluminum throughout

- Fabrication: CubCrafters facility, night shift production run
- Each carbon fiber component produced from CNC-machined molds
- Assembly joints designed for flat-pack shipping and driveway installation


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STRUCTURAL DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FLAT-PACK / MODULAR ASSEMBLY
- Topper ships in sections sized for standard freight
- All joints are structural and visually seamless when assembled
- Assembly achievable by one person with basic hand tools
- Components indexed so assembly is foolproof — panels only fit one way
- Joints designed with same philosophy as aircraft section assembly

TOP PANEL
- Single carbon fiber panel following the R1S roofline spline
- Integrated mounting rail interface matching Rivian factory crossbar spec
  (same attachment system used on the R1T cab section, extended aft)
- Two crossbar positions on top surface — forward and aft of bed center
- Top surface completely uninterrupted — no cutouts, no exposed hardware

SIDE PANEL FRAMES
- Structural frames run the length of the bed on each side
- Each frame contains multiple slots accepting interchangeable insert modules
- Consistent slot/locking interface shared across all module types
- Three insert positions per side (approximate — confirm against bed length)

REAR DOOR
- Full-width swing-up door, gas-strut assisted
- Flush hinges integrated into carbon fiber panel
- Seals against topper frame when closed
- Houses brake light and rear camera (see below)

BASE RAIL / MOUNTING SYSTEM
- Precision-machined brackets engage Rivian factory 500lb-rated bed anchor points
- No drilling into vehicle required
- Tool-free quick release
- Topper removes as a unit or in sections


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MODULAR SIDE PANEL INSERT SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Each side slot accepts one of the following insert types. Buyers configure at
purchase and can swap modules post-sale.

MODULE TYPES (v1 planned):

1. MESH / SCREEN
   - Ventilated panel for airflow
   - Primary panel for dog canopy use case
   - UV-resistant mesh material

2. TINTED OR CLEAR WINDOW
   - Fixed glazing for weather protection with visibility
   - Flush fit within carbon frame

3. RECOVERY BOARD MOUNT
   - External mounting surface for MaxTrax, TRED Pro, or equivalent
   - Flush to topper side profile when boards loaded

4. STRAP / TOW GEAR ORGANIZER
   - External accessible storage for recovery straps, tow lines
   - Accessed from outside without opening topper

5. SIDE-OPENING TOOL CUBBY
   - Swing-up lid with gas strut, accessed from the side
   - Internal storage for tools, work site gear
   - Lid stores flush to side panel when open
   - Locking latch when closed


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
INTEGRATED FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

BUTTON RELOCATION PANEL
- Rivian R1T has control buttons on the top plane of the bed rail surface
  (gear tunnel access, tonneau cover, tailgate controls)
- Topper covers these buttons; mechanical extensions relocate all controls
  to a flush panel on the side of the topper
- Relocation panel positioned at an ergonomic reach height on the side
- Top carbon surface remains completely uninterrupted

INTERIOR LIGHTING
- Flush LED strip or panel recessed into carbon fiber ceiling
- Full bed area illumination
- Use cases: dog visibility, gear access, camping/overnight

BRAKE LIGHT
- LED light bar integrated into rear door face
- Mimics R1S taillight signature styling
- DOT compliant
- Wired through bed to vehicle lighting circuit
- Required by law as topper covers OEM rear lighting

REAR CAMERA
- Flush-mounted camera housing in rear door
- Wired to tap into Rivian's existing rear camera harness
- Restores full reverse camera functionality blocked by topper
- Camera positioned at equivalent height to OEM unit
- Note: Rivian camera harness integration may require community
  workaround or Rivian API access — design conduit and mount from day one
  regardless of integration path

CROSSBAR MOUNTS
- Two sets of Rivian-compatible crossbar mounting rails on top surface
- Identical interface to factory cab crossbar mounts
- Allows owners to extend existing Rivian crossbar accessories aft
- Owners with crossbars on cab can replicate same configuration on topper


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DOG CANOPY — LAUNCH USE CASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Mesh side panels provide full ventilation on all sides
- Interior LED lighting for visibility and comfort
- Swing-up rear door for dog entry/exit
- Optional food and water dish mounts (attachment points built into base rail)
- Sized for one medium dog (approx 40–60 lbs reference animal: female
  Timber Wolf / Husky mix)
- Shade provided by carbon fiber top panel

OPEN ITEMS / DEPENDENCIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. RIVIAN COLLABORATION (recommended)
   - Request official CAD reference geometry for R1T bed and R1S roofline
   - Confirm crossbar mounting rail specification
   - Confirm bed anchor point load ratings and interface dimensions
   - Explore rear camera harness connector specification
   - Rivian benefits: extends accessory ecosystem, fills R1T/R1S gap,
     reflects well on platform

2. PHYSICAL MEASUREMENT
   - If Rivian CAD unavailable, measure reference R1T and R1S vehicles directly
   - Critical: bed rail height, cab roofline height at rear, bed length/width,
     anchor point positions, button positions on bed rail top surface

3. SOLIDWORKS DEVELOPMENT SEQUENCE
   a. Master reference sketch — R1T bed geometry as base
   b. R1S roofline spline mapped onto R1T bed profile (the master curve)
   c. Top panel surface model
   d. Side panel frame family of parts with shared module interface
   e. Rear door assembly with hinge and gas strut geometry
   f. Button relocation panel and mechanical extension design
   g. Camera mount housing
   h. Brake light housing and wiring path
   i. Interior light recess
   j. Quick-release base bracket matched to Rivian anchor spec
   k. Crossbar mount rail interface
   l. Module library (each insert type as sub-assembly)
   m. Flat-pack joint design and assembly sequence

4. REGULATORY
   - Brake light must meet DOT requirements
   - Camera integration legality by state (confirm)
   - Structural load rating for crossbar mounts with cargo

5. FUTURE TOPPER ECOSYSTEM (same base shell)
   - Hard shell fully enclosed carbon topper
   - Pop-top / rooftop tent platform
   - Overlanding / weather sealed expedition shell
   - Sleeper pod configuration
